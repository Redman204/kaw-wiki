NEW PLAYER/BUILD BASICS
🏗Open all 25 Lowlands which will unlock silver bars.

🌐Next open 50 lands in total with minimum level 1 builds which opens up the blitz bonus. Since abyss lands is next gold open highlands up to hoarfrost until abyss feature is available 

🌎Next you want to open all 25 Abyss lands which unlocks gold bars

💰Sell all silver bars to get more lands & use the crestplates for building & upgrades from there.
🌀Next, beat the side legends & get all 300 points on the epic battle blitz rounds.
🤪750 legends = free crestplates silver bars and even gold bars
🤼‍♂️Always check your max plunder (MP) till you get it dialed in.
MP comes from hiring allies. Its not about how many but how much they cost.
🏗Every time you upgrade, recheck MP. To find your new MP, add all your stats and multiply by 5,500.
🛖After initial volley you will have 14 to 15 lands open go to market place and buy sleeping potion (depraved pig eb) and mist of confusion (to the chamber pig eb) both first bar items the bonus after eb ends will provide enough gold to finish buying lowlands
❄️On hoarfrost lands there is a glitch that if you have a level 1 attack build and a level 1 spy build you will have extra troops at 100% also takes longer than a hour to fully regen never upgrade them two level one builds
